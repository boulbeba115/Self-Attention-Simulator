    What you are looking at

    A teaching model, not a product. Here is exactly how far it goes.

    01

    The maths is real.

    Embeddings, attention, masking, softmax, layer normalisation and sampling all work the way they do in GPT-style models. They are just small enough here that every number fits on screen, and you can click any of them to see how it was worked out.

    02

    The knowledge is counted, not trained.

    A real model learns by adjusting billions of weights. This one has random weights, so its knowledge comes from counting which word followed which phrase in its reading. Turn “Training-text strength” down to zero in the settings and you will see the network on its own: it babbles.

    03

    So it is not ChatGPT.

    It repeats what it has read and falls apart outside it. Ask it for the capital of France and it knows; ask it why, and it does not. That is the point: it is small enough to watch, one step at a time.
